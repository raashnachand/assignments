# Outsourcing Data to the Philippines

Worked in a team of four to determine if it was appropriate for a New Zealand-based organisation to outsource the processing and storage of personal customer information to the Philippines.

* Researched Filipino and New Zealand data privacy laws.
* Considered international regulations, guidelines, and frameworks for our decision.
* Addressed the ethical implications of not notifying customers of the decision to offshore.

My part of the presentation is slides 10-19 in `Outsourcing-Data-to-the-Philippines.pdf`, and Part A in `Assignment-brief.pdf`.
